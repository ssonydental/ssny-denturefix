● Common Denture Problems ●

A Denture Rarely Breaks at a Good Time.

A hairline crack you keep an eye on. A tooth that finally pops off. A fit that's gone loose over the years. By the time it snaps, you're stuck without it. We look at all of it, and repair what we can the same visit:

  • Cracked or Split Denture Base

    A crack down the pink base only gets wider with each bite until it splits for good.

    Bring the denture in early. A clean crack is one of the most repairable problems, often the same day in our on-site lab.

  • Popped-Off Denture Tooth

    A tooth that has come off the denture leaves an obvious gap and a sharp edge.

    Keep the tooth and bring it with you. Reattaching a denture tooth is usually a quick same-day fix.

  • Broken Clean in Half

    A denture snapped fully in two often breaks along an old repair or a weak spot.

    Bring every piece. We’ll assess whether it can be rejoined to last, or whether a replacement is the smarter call.

  • Loose or Slipping Denture

    A denture that clicks, slips, or won’t stay put usually means the fit has changed over time.

    Often solved with a reline rather than a full replacement. Getting it looked at early keeps it from cracking or causing sores.

  • Sore Spots & Rubbing

    Raw spots, rubbing, or a denture that suddenly hurts to wear usually points to a fit that’s off.

    An adjustment or reline often fixes it. Our lab is on site, so many fit issues are handled in the same visit.

  • Broken Clasp on a Partial

    A bent or snapped metal clasp lets a partial wobble and stops it gripping your natural teeth.

    We assess whether the clasp can be repaired or the partial re-fitted so it holds securely again.

  • Worn-Out Denture

    Flattened teeth, staining, or a base that keeps breaking often means the denture has simply run its course.

    We’ll tell you honestly when repairs aren’t worth it and start a replacement through our lab.

  • Chipped Denture Tooth

    A chipped or worn front tooth on a denture shows in every photo and catches on your lip.

    Cosmetic chips can often be smoothed or the tooth rebuilt in the same visit at our on-site lab.

  • Failed DIY Glue Repair

    Super glue or drugstore kits rarely hold and can warp the fit so it never sits right again.

    Don’t keep re-gluing. Bring it in and we’ll assess whether a proper repair is still possible.

  • New Denture Won’t Fit

    A recent or immediate denture that pinches, rocks, or won’t seat often just needs adjusting in.

    Early fit problems are normal and usually solved with an adjustment or reline, not a whole new denture.

  • Any Other Denture Problem

    Not sure what’s wrong with it? You don’t need to know the fix to bring it in.

    Book a consultation and we’ll take a look. Assessment, panoramic image and 3D CBCT scan are included.

● Denture Repair FAQ ●

What Patients Ask Us Most.

+Can you fix a broken denture the same day?

Often, yes. Many cracks, snapped teeth, and clean breaks can be repaired the same day in our on-site lab. We start with a scheduled consultation to see exactly what broke. Some dentures can be repaired, and others are better replaced. Either way, you'll leave with a clear plan.

+What kinds of denture problems do you handle?

A cracked or split denture base, a tooth that popped off the denture, a loose or ill-fitting denture, a broken clasp on a partial, or a denture that's worn out and no longer sits right. If it broke or stopped fitting, it's worth getting looked at.

+Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Denture repairs start with a scheduled consultation so we can assess the denture and your fit before any work begins. Book online or call and we'll get you in as soon as we can.

+Should I try to glue my denture back together myself?

It's best not to. Household and store-bought glues can warp the fit, are hard to reverse, and often make a proper repair harder. Keep all the pieces, bring them in, and let us assess it. That gives you the best chance at a clean repair.

+What happens if my denture can't be repaired?

Some dentures are too worn or broken to repair well. If that's the case, we'll tell you honestly and start a replacement through our lab, usually about a one-week turnaround, so you're not left guessing.

+My denture doesn't hurt, it just won't stay in. Is that worth a visit?

Yes. A loose or slipping denture usually means the fit has changed and often needs a reline rather than a full replacement. Getting it assessed early is cheaper and easier than waiting until it cracks or causes sores.

+What are your hours?

Friday 9:30am to 5pm, Saturday 8am to 10pm, Sunday 9am to 5pm, and Monday 6:30am to 5pm. Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are by appointment only. Denture repair visits are scheduled, so book online or call for a time.

+What if I can't pay for treatment right now?

Cost shouldn't keep you stuck without a working denture. We offer 0% financing for all credit types, so you can get seen and handle payment over time.
